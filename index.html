The through line

In a cooperative the value chain is a circle

Milk leaves the farm, gains value at every stage, and the result returns to the same farms as a milk price. I have stood on most of those stages. The dashed part is where I want to work next.

  1. Farm

    Grew up on a dairy farm, and kept going back

    Austria from the start. Later a season on an Arla owner's farm in Denmark, running milk production and herd management. I still stand in for farming families when illness stops them.

  2. Processing

    Alpine cheese from raw milk

    Switzerland, from curd handling to ripening, and the full daily cycle of on-farm processing.

  3. Across the chain

    Costing and profitability for dairy companies

    In consulting I own the platform market-leading dairy companies use to see what a product actually costs, and the client relationships behind it.

  4. Trade & finance

    Arla Global Trading and Planning Finance

    Investment business cases, a cost and benefit governance framework, and a production cost variance analysis that corrected a six-figure reporting deviation.

  5. Next · the return leg

    Carrying the result back to the farm

    Post-merger integration with DMK Group. Shortening the path from data to decision. Net working capital, the subject of my master's thesis. Carbon and sustainability figures, which move into controlling as CSRD applies from 2027.

Everything above the dashed step has happened. The dashed step is what I want to contribute, so that the farmer owners get a result they can plan and invest with.

Motivation

Why Arla, and why this programme

An industry that will always be needed

People will always eat, in one form or another. I want to spend my working life somewhere that stays necessary, and where what I calculate has a physical counterpart I can stand next to.

A company that brings people together

A cooperative that takes its democratic structure seriously and uses it to connect people, that aims to lead rather than follow, and that lives sustainability rather than reporting it. A conversation with the chair of the board showed me what it means to belong to Arla as a farmer. Friday breakfast with the team showed me how much people there want to be there.

Carrying data into decisions

2025 showed how quickly the ground can move: firm prices in the first half, a sharp correction in the second. Steering a cooperative through that needs figures that are ready before the decision, not after it. That is the work I want to do, so the farmer owners get a result they can plan and invest with.

Three rotations, and an honest self-assessment

  • 1Business and Performance — closest to what I already do, and even there I keep running into parts of how a cooperative is steered that I have not seen.
  • 2Data and Digital — builds on what I brought into consulting, in a field where the tools change faster than anyone can follow on their own.
  • 3Strategy and Execution — where I have the least experience and the most to learn.

I would rather spend two years where I can be useful from the start and still be learning at the end.
